Before you contact us

Which information should I include?

For tool-related problems, include the name of the tool and briefly explain what happened. This helps us understand your issue.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Yes. You can use the contact form to send ideas or suggestions for tools and improvements you would like to see on Weblixa.

Can I report a website problem?

Yes. Please describe the problem clearly and, if possible, mention the page or tool where you experienced it.

How should I provide my email?

Use an email address that you can access. This allows Weblixa to reply to your message when a response is necessary.
