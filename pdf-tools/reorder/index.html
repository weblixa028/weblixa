PDF Pages

Drag pages to change their order.

How to use:
1. Upload a PDF file.
2. Your PDF pages will appear on the screen.
3. Drag and drop pages into your desired order.
4. You can also use the ↑ and ↓ buttons.
5. Click "Create Reordered PDF".
6. Click "Download Reordered PDF".

Your PDF is processed in the browser. No PDF upload to a Weblixa server is required.
